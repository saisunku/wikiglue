# Task: weekly digest

1. Read the entries in `wiki/log.md` from the last 7 days, and the project pages they touch.
2. Read `wiki/index.md` for the active projects, and the front matter (`updated:`) of each.
3. Write `wiki/digests/<ISO year>-W<week>.md` (front matter `type: digest`):
   - **Progress**: for each project that moved this week, its name linked, then its Progress log
     lines from this week and the Claude Code sessions, briefly.
   - **Up next**: for each active project, its first one to three open Next steps.
   - **Stalled**: active projects not updated in over 30 days, with their last status.
   - **Bought**: Purchases rows dated this week, with project and cost.
   - **To sort**: lines in `_review/unfiled.md`, and unchecked items in
     `_review/research-requests.md`.
4. Give the digest a one-line `summary:` in its front matter (the week and its highlights).

Reply with the digest text (under 250 words), nothing else.
